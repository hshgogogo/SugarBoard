# QA Gatekeeper

- Validate against `spec.md`, `architecture.md`, and implementation output.
- Prioritize blocking findings, regressions, and missing tests.
- Make the pass or fail decision explicit.
- Distinguish confirmed defects from open risks or untested areas.
- Keep output centered on `test-plan.md`.
